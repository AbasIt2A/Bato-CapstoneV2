# High Blood Pressure in the Philippines

## What counts as high blood pressure in the Philippines

In the Philippines, high blood pressure (hypertension) is defined as a blood
pressure of **140/90 mmHg or higher** (PSH, 2020).

## Why the Philippine threshold is 140/90 and not 130/80

Nearly all international guidelines define hypertension as 140/90 mmHg or
above; the American AHA/ACC guideline is the exception, setting it at 130/80.
The 2020 Philippine guideline deliberately kept 140/90, unchanged from the
2011 Philippine guideline, because there was no compelling reason to change
it — and because Philippine national surveys and prevalence studies all use
that definition, so changing it would have confused disease surveillance
(PSH, 2020).

So a Philippine reading and an American one can classify the same person
differently. If you have seen the 130/80 figure quoted somewhere, that is why.
Ask your own doctor or health centre which threshold they are using for you.

## Why high blood pressure matters this much

Hypertension is the most common cause of death and disability worldwide, and
its prevalence is rising in low- and middle-income countries. It remains an
important cause of illness and death in the Philippines, with poor blood
pressure control being one of the main reasons (PSH, 2020).

For kidney health specifically, high blood pressure is one of the two leading
causes of chronic kidney disease, and it is one of the nine things the
screening questionnaire asks about.

## Kidney disease as a reason to take blood pressure seriously

The Philippine guideline treats the presence of chronic kidney disease or
diabetes — or any evidence that other organs are being affected — as a factor
that changes how hypertension is assessed and followed up (PSH, 2020). It
gives separate management guidance for people with hypertension who also have
diabetes, chronic kidney disease, or a history of stroke, and for pregnant
women and children.

What those separate plans should be is a matter for a doctor. BATO does not
give blood pressure targets or medicine advice.

## Who wrote this guidance

The 2020 Philippine Clinical Practice Guidelines on Hypertension were
developed collaboratively by Philippine medical societies and groups —
including the National Kidney and Transplant Institute — to give Filipino
health professionals a single local, evidence-based reference for diagnosing,
treating and following up people with hypertension (PSH, 2020).
