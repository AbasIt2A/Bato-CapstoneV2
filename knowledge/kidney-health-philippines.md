# Kidney Health in the Philippines

## CKD as a leading cause of death in the Philippines

Kidney disease is consistently among the leading causes of death in the
Philippines (DOH).

## How many people die of kidney disease in the Philippines

An estimated **39,000 deaths every year** in the Philippines are
attributable to kidney disease (DOH, 2026).

This is an estimate, not an exact count. Deaths are officially recorded by
their primary coded cause, so many people who had chronic kidney disease are
counted under heart disease or diabetes instead — the true toll of kidney
disease is therefore higher than any single line in a mortality table
suggests.

## How many Filipinos develop kidney failure

Roughly one Filipino develops kidney (renal) failure every hour — about 120
new cases per million population each year (PSN, 2024, citing National
Kidney and Transplant Institute data).

## How many Filipinos are on dialysis

More than **64,000 Filipinos** relied on dialysis treatment in 2024 (DOH,
2026). Dialysis need has been rising, which is why the Department of Health
and the Philippine Society of Nephrology both stress earlier detection —
CKD found in its early stages can often be slowed before it reaches the
stage where dialysis is required.

## National Kidney Month

The Department of Health observes National Kidney Month every June to
promote awareness and the early detection of kidney disease (DOH).

## Role of the Philippine Society of Nephrology

The Philippine Society of Nephrology (PSN) is the professional
organization of Filipino kidney specialists. It accredits dialysis
centers and promotes kidney health education (PSN).

## PhilHealth coverage for dialysis

PhilHealth provides benefit packages that help cover the cost of
dialysis. Coverage details change over time, so ask PhilHealth or a
local health center for the current benefits (DOH).

## Kidney disease burden and early detection in the Philippines

Kidney disease is a significant and growing health problem in the
Philippines. The Department of Health and the Philippine Society of
Nephrology encourage earlier detection, especially for people with
diabetes or high blood pressure (DOH).
