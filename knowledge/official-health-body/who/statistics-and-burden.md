# Global Statistics and Burden of Kidney Disease

## Global burden of kidney disease

Kidney disease is one of the few leading causes of death still rising
worldwide. It climbed from the 19th leading cause of death globally in 2000
to the 9th in 2021, with the number of deaths increasing by about 95% over
that period (WHO).

Exact worldwide death counts change with each new estimate release, so BATO
does not quote a single global death total — the trend (rising, now inside
the global top 10) is the reliable takeaway.

Philippine figures are covered separately under Kidney Health in the
Philippines.
