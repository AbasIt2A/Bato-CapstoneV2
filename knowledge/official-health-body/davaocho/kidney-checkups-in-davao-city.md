# Getting Your Kidneys Checked in Davao City

## Where to get your kidneys checked in Davao City

Davao City's district health centres offer free medical consultations, and the
City Health Office encourages Dabawenyos to use them (Davao City CHO, 2026).

A person who wants their kidneys checked can go to the health centre serving
their own district. Confirm the schedule and what to bring with that centre
directly — this guidance covers what is offered, not the day-to-day
arrangements of any one centre.

## What tests the Davao City health centres can run

The city's main health centres have their own laboratories and are able to run
the basic tests used to look at kidney health (Davao City CHO, 2026):

- creatinine
- urinalysis
- complete blood count
- cholesterol
- uric acid

Creatinine and urinalysis are the two that speak most directly to how the
kidneys are working.

The City Health Office frames this as deliberately broad — the aim is to see as
complete a picture as possible so that a patient can be treated (Davao City
CHO, 2026).

## Why the City Health Office urges early consultation

The City Health Office's position is that early detection is key to saving
lives and to reducing long-term health costs (Davao City CHO, 2026).

The cost argument is a practical one for Filipino households: the CHO's own
framing is that investing in a healthier lifestyle now is cheaper than paying
for maintenance medicines later (Davao City CHO, 2026).

## Kidney disease is appearing in younger Dabawenyos

Patients with kidney disease in Davao City are becoming younger, a trend the
City Health Office links to poor eating habits, consumption of processed food,
and lifestyle risks (Davao City CHO, 2026).

Being young is therefore not a reason to skip a check if other risk factors are
present.

## Conditions the City Health Office links to kidney damage locally

High blood pressure, diabetes and high uric acid can all damage the kidneys if
they are left untreated — but they can be managed effectively when they are
detected early (Davao City CHO, 2026).

The City Health Office also warns that both smoking and vaping contribute to
kidney damage, and advises eating in moderation and keeping properly hydrated
(Davao City CHO, 2026).
