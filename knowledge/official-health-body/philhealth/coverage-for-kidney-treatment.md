# PhilHealth Coverage for Dialysis and Kidney Transplant

## How much PhilHealth covers for one dialysis session

As of 30 August 2026, PhilHealth's case rate for hemodialysis is **₱6,350 per
session** (PhilHealth).

The benefit is capped at **90 days per year**, and one session counts as one
day against that limit (PhilHealth).

Rates and limits are revised by circular. Confirm the current amount with
PhilHealth or a local health centre before relying on it.

## What the dialysis benefit covers

As of 30 August 2026, the hemodialysis benefit (PhilHealth):

- covers both inpatient and outpatient procedures, including emergency dialysis
  for acute renal failure
- may be claimed as a second case rate, at the full amount
- is exempt from the Single Period of Confinement rule
- is available at **all accredited facilities** — it is no longer restricted to
  hospitals and freestanding dialysis centres, as long as the service is within
  the facility's DOH licence

Multiple sessions may be filed on a single claim form.

## Peritoneal dialysis and kidney transplant under Z Benefits

PhilHealth covers peritoneal dialysis and kidney transplantation under its Z
Benefits packages, which are the packages for catastrophic illnesses
(PhilHealth).

Peritoneal dialysis packages exist for both adults and paediatric patients.
Continuous Ambulatory Peritoneal Dialysis (CAPD) is for people diagnosed with
CKD stage 5 who need kidney replacement therapy; it requires a permanent
catheter, completed PD initiation at an accredited facility, and a patient or
caregiver trained to do the exchanges at home (PhilHealth).

## How much the kidney transplant packages cover

As of 30 August 2026, PhilHealth's kidney transplantation packages range from
about **₱865,000 to ₱2,146,000**, depending on the type of transplant
(PhilHealth):

- Living organ donor, open surgery for donor and recipient: **₱865,000**
- Living organ donor, laparoscopic donor surgery: **₱993,000** to
  **₱1,045,000**
- Deceased organ donor: **₱1,583,000** to **₱2,146,000**, depending on how the
  organ was retrieved and which medicine is used

Whether a person qualifies for any of these is decided by a transplant centre
against detailed medical criteria, not by the patient. Confirm current amounts
and requirements with PhilHealth or an accredited transplant facility.

## Where to confirm your own coverage

Benefit packages, amounts and eligibility rules change over time (PhilHealth).

Ask PhilHealth directly, or ask a local health centre or the billing section of
an accredited facility, for the benefits that apply to a specific person on a
specific date. Nothing here is a guarantee of what an individual will receive.
