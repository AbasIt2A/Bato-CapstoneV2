# Kidney Imaging and Biopsy

## Kidney ultrasound and CT scans

Kidney imaging can help a health professional look for a blockage, stone, cyst, tumor, or another urinary tract problem. Ultrasound uses sound waves, has no radiation exposure, and can show whether the kidney has a blockage, stone, or tumor. A CT scan uses X-rays and can show stones, blockages, infections, cysts, tumors, and injuries; lower-radiation-dose CT can be used for urinary stones. The right test depends on the symptoms, medical history, and what the clinician needs to see. Imaging alone does not explain every abnormal kidney blood or urine test.

Source: https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diagnostic-tests/urinary-tract-imaging

## Contrast dye and kidney function

Some imaging tests use a contrast dye to make details easier to see. Contrast can cause kidney injury in some people, including those with impaired kidney function; most people have no injury or only a temporary change, while lasting injury is rare. Tell the imaging team about kidney disease, diabetes, past reactions, medicines, and any fluid restriction so they can choose the test and precautions. Kidney function is checked with laboratory tests. The risk depends on the person's condition and the planned test, so a chatbot cannot decide whether a specific contrast scan is safe for an individual.

Source: https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diagnostic-tests/urinary-tract-imaging

## Why a kidney biopsy is done and what to expect

A kidney biopsy removes a tiny tissue sample to help identify kidney damage or its cause when blood and urine tests are not enough. A clinician may consider it for persistent blood or too much protein in urine, or unexplained kidney function problems. Before the procedure, review medicines and supplements with the care team because aspirin, NSAIDs, and blood thinners may raise bleeding risk. Bleeding and soreness are the main risks; infection is rare. After a biopsy, seek medical care promptly for dark red urine or clots, worsening pain, fever, or faintness.
