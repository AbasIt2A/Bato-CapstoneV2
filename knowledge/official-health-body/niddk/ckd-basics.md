# CKD Basics

## What the kidneys do

The kidneys filter waste products and extra water out of the blood, which
leaves the body as urine. They also help control blood pressure, keep bones
healthy, and help make red blood cells (NIDDK).

## What CKD is

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) means the kidneys are damaged or have been
filtering blood poorly for 3 months or longer (KDIGO definition: reduced
kidney function, GFR below 60 mL/min/1.73m², or markers of kidney damage).

CKD usually develops slowly and silently. Most people have NO symptoms in
the early stages — many only find out when the disease is advanced (CDC).

CKD cannot usually be cured, but early detection and treatment can slow or
stop it from getting worse (NIDDK, WHO).
