# What Kidney Tests Cost at a Davao Private Hospital

## What a creatinine test and urinalysis cost

At Davao Doctors Hospital, a private hospital in Davao City, the laboratory
price list as of **24 March 2025** gave these cash prices (DDH, 2025):

- Creatinine (blood test): **₱354**
- Urinalysis (automated): **₱200**

These two are the tests most directly used to look at kidney function and for
protein in the urine.

Prices change. Confirm the current amount with the laboratory before going.

## What the other common kidney-related tests cost

From the same Davao Doctors Hospital list, as of **24 March 2025**, cash prices
(DDH, 2025):

- Blood urea nitrogen (BUN / urea): **₱367**
- Creatinine clearance: **₱540**
- Microalbumin–creatinine ratio, 24-hour urine: **₱750**
- Uric acid: **₱453**
- Albumin: **₱392**

A test billed to an HMO is listed at a slightly higher price than the cash
price. The list also carries lower "service case" rates for some tests —
whether a person qualifies for one is decided by the hospital, not by this
guide (DDH, 2025).

## These are one hospital's prices, not the price everywhere

Everything above is the published tariff of a single private hospital in Davao
City as of 24 March 2025 (DDH, 2025).

Costs differ between facilities. A government district health centre, another
private hospital, and a standalone laboratory will each charge differently for
the same test — and Davao City's district health centres offer free
consultations. Treat these figures as a rough sense of scale for private
laboratory testing, and ask the facility a person actually plans to visit.
