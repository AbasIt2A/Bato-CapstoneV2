# Glomerulonephritis

## What glomerulonephritis is, and how it is checked

Glomerulonephritis is inflammation and scarring of the kidneys' tiny blood
filters, called glomeruli. It has several possible causes, including some
infections and immune conditions such as lupus or IgA nephropathy. Signs may
include blood or protein in urine, swelling, and high blood pressure, but
these do not establish a diagnosis. A health professional checks blood and
urine and may need a kidney biopsy. Treatment depends on the underlying cause
and how much kidney damage has occurred.
